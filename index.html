About

Energy Transition by day, exploring London by night.

I'm a Manager on EY's Energy Transition team in London, where I work with utilities, manufacturers, and government clients on the strategy, program design, and operations behind decarbonization, from customer programs and clean energy portfolios to the technology that runs underneath them.

I joined EY in the US in January 2021 and transferred to EY UK&I in 2026, moving from New York to London in the process.

Outside of work, I mentor through the Clean Energy Leadership Institute, Young Professionals in Energy, and Minds Matter, volunteer at Migrateful, and write two Substacks, one professional, one personal.

When I'm not working, I like to explore London by talking to just about anybody and reading up on local history.

      EY, Energy Transition Consulting

      2021–Present

      I work within EY's Customer practice on the energy transition, leading program design, portfolio strategy, and market analysis for utilities, manufacturers, and government clients as they build out clean energy and customer programs. Client and engagement details below are generalized.

      Alongside client work, I have served as a deputy leader for building out a Customer Utilities practice in the US and I am beginning a similar role in the UK. This involves developing internal strategy, external campaigns, internal enablement assets, and delivering a capability upskilling program.

      Selected engagements

      • Led discovery for a customer debt-transformation program, running workshops and stakeholder interviews to shape a business case and roadmap for the client's investment decision.
      • Led a rapid-response initiative addressing a CEO-level priority in customer credit and collections, redesigning the operating model to drive measurable savings.
      • Served as delivery lead on a large, multi-team public-sector program, scaling it from a pilot to a statewide rollout.
      • Shaped a regulated utility's growth strategy through market research, competitive benchmarking, and customer segmentation.
      • Led financial, marketing, and change-management workstreams to launch new residential product offerings, from concept through regulatory filing.
      • Evaluated and optimized a clean energy program portfolio, from current-state assessment through the launch of a new program.
      • Built a scenario-based prioritization framework and financial model to align capital initiatives across regulatory jurisdictions.
