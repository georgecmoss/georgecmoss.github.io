About

Energy Transition by day, exploring by night.

I'm a Manager on EY's Energy Transition team in London, where I work with utilities, manufacturers, and government clients on the strategy, program design, and operations behind decarbonization, from customer programs and clean energy portfolios to the technology that runs underneath them.

I studied Commerce (IT & Business Analytics) and Global Development Studies at the University of Virginia.

Outside of work, I mentor through the Clean Energy Leadership Institute, Young Professionals in Energy, and Minds Matter, volunteer at Migrateful, and write two Substacks, one professional, one personal. I'm also into urban livability, gardening, and personal finance, and I speak conversational Greek and Spanish.
