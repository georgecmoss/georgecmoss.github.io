How I Learn

A few habits that stick

I learn best by writing about what I'm trying to understand, which is a big part of why I keep two newsletters going. Turning a half-formed idea into a few paragraphs forces me to actually figure out what I think.

Outside of that: a short daily reading habit (industry news plus a book on the go), conversations with people doing the work firsthand through mentoring and volunteering, and revisiting notes rather than just taking them.
