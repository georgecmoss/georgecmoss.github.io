How I Learn

A few habits that stick

I learn best by writing about what I'm trying to understand, which is a big part of why I keep two newsletters going. Turning a half-formed idea into a few paragraphs forces me to actually figure out what I think. The rest comes from a fairly steady set of routines:

  • Daily

    Journaling, blogging, mindfulness, and exercise, with some vibe coding thrown in lately.

  • Weekly

    A standing date night with my wife.

  • Monthly

    Revisiting old journal entries, plus James Clear's newsletter.

  • Bi-annual

    Budgeting, twice a year.

  • Yearly

    A full review of the year.

Outside of that structure: conversations with people doing the work firsthand, through mentoring and volunteering.
